Population Impact

  • TNR / TNVR (Trap-Neuter-Return / Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return) reduces community-cat numbers — models need ~70–75% of cats sterilised to stabilise a colony; targeted TNR cut shelter intake ~66% from the targeted zip code.+
