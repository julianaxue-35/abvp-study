Funding Structures: Government vs. Non-Profit

▢ to fill from your notes

  • Government / municipal funding (tax revenue · open-admission mandate)
  • Private non-profit (501(c)(3) — grants, donations, earned revenue)
  • 501(c)(3) compliance (exempt purpose · no private inurement · limited lobbying)
  • Form 990 — must be on public display at ALL times, not "upon request"+

Grants, Restricted Funds, and Donor Intent

▢ to fill from your notes

  • Restricted vs. unrestricted grants
  • General operating support (GOS — unrestricted, most valued)
  • Grant management best practice (separate accounting, reporting, stewardship)
  • No emergency exception — restricted funds cannot be diverted, even in an outbreak (e.g. TNR (trap-neuter-return) money to buy parvovirus PPE)+

Board of Directors Fiscal Oversight

▢ to fill from your notes

  • Board duties (governance, fiscal oversight, strategy)
  • What the board does NOT do (day-to-day ops, medical protocols)
  • Fiscal oversight in practice (approve budget, audit, Form 990)
  • CEO is the only staff the board directly hires — board governs, staff operates+

Resource Allocation and Staff Utilization

▢ to fill from your notes

  • Allocation framework (staff hrs, kennels, supplies, budget vs. C4C (capacity for care))
  • Staff conflict & operational health (turnover, compassion fatigue)
  • NACA (National Animal Care & Control Assoc.) minimum = 15 min/animal/day, feeding + cleaning ONLY — a floor, not a target+

Budget Planning Basics

▢ to fill from your notes

  • Operating budget (fixed · variable · capital costs)
  • In-kind donations (fair market value, Form 990, donor intent applies)
  • Cost-per-live-outcome (CPLO)
  • Capital ≠ operating — capital expenditure needs separate board approval above a threshold+
  • Low CPLO is not always better — pair with welfare indicators (length of stay (LOS), disease rate)+
