📄 Journal Article

  • Owner survey after a canine leptospirosis outbreak (LA County, 2021): only 22% of dogs were vaccinated against leptospirosis; 77% of owners were unaware of lepto's clinical signs or its zoonotic nature; and 68% were unaware of the outbreak.
  • Leptospira venezuelensis outbreak (Venezuela): an intermediate-species Leptospira (L. venezuelensis) spread via local rodents to dairy cows and humans within a region already endemic for L. interrogans — a reminder that intermediate / novel species circulate alongside the classic ones.
  • Leptospira seroprevalence in dogs & cats (meta-analysis, 84 studies): overall prevalence differed significantly between dogs and cats; in dogs, L. interrogans serogroup Canicola predominated, and L. kirschneri serogroup Grippotyphosa was higher in stray/sheltered than owned dogs. Pathogenic serogroups from small mammals circulate in asymptomatic pets — include both dogs and cats as reservoirs and in the MAT diagnostic panel.
