## Coverage Gaps

- **No 2021 issues:** JSMCAH did not exist before 2022 (launched by ASV in 2022)
- **Vol. 5 (2026) is ongoing:** Only articles published through June 2026 are captured; future 2026 articles will not appear until later
- **ABVP abstract-only articles:** Several articles appear in both ABVP abstract issues (S1) and later as full papers in regular issues; the dedupe list already captures both the abstract and full-paper versions of most of these
- **PDF-only content:** Several special-issue guideline documents (ASV Standards of Care 2nd Ed., Rabbit Housing Guidelines, Principles of Vet Community Engagement) were included in the journal but are essentially single-document PDFs rather than peer-reviewed research articles; the Principles of VCE (view/76) was included in jsmcah.json as it has accessible abstract-quality content
- **Opinion articles without abstracts:** Four opinion/critique articles (view/28, view/68, view/120, view/154) have no formal abstracts on the journal platform; per rules, these were excluded from jsmcah.json
