Intestinal Endoparasites

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Nematodes — hook / round / whip; cestodes — tapeworms
  • Zoonotic: Toxocara (roundworm) & Ancylostoma (hookworm) cause larva migrans — visceral / ocular & cutaneous+
  • Diagnosis — faecal float; note the pre-patent period

Giardia duodenalis

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Treat: fenbendazole; combine drug + bathe the coat + clean/dry the environment to stop re-infection+
  • Signs — small-bowel diarrhoea; often subclinical carriers
  • Other protozoa — Cystoisospora (coccidia), Cryptosporidium, Tritrichomonas foetus (cats)

Heartworm Disease

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Agent — Dirofilaria immitis, mosquito-transmitted
  • Prevention: start monthly heartworm preventive; test (antigen ± microfilaria) before starting in endemic areas+
  • Treatment — AHS (American Heartworm Society) adulticide protocol; staging & exercise restriction

Ectoparasites

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Sarcoptes (sarcoptic mange) = zoonotic & intensely pruritic; scrape has low sensitivity → treat on suspicion. Demodex = non-contagious (immune / stress-related)+
  • Ear mites — Otodectes cynotis (dark ceruminous "coffee-ground" debris)
  • Lice — host-specific; diagnosis — skin scrape / tape / otoscope

Fleas

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Flea = intermediate host of Dipylidium caninum (tapeworm) → treat fleas and deworm+
  • Disease — FAD (flea-allergy dermatitis), anaemia in small / young animals
  • Flea-borne — Bartonella, Rickettsia, haemotropic Mycoplasma

Tick biology and management

▢ to fill from your notes
  • Ticks transmit Babesia, Ehrlichia, Anaplasma; remove promptly + apply an acaricide+
  • Removal — grasp at skin, steady traction; avoid leaving mouthparts
  • Life cycle — larva → nymph → adult; each needs a blood meal

Deworming schedule
